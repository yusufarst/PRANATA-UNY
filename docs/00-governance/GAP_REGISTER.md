## GAP-resolution operating track

This track organizes resolution work. It is not a P11 Task list and never changes canonical GAP status by itself. Track states: NOT_STARTED, ASSIGNED, EVIDENCE_REQUESTED, EVIDENCE_RECEIVED, UNDER_REVIEW, RESOLVED, ACCEPTED_DEFERRAL, CLOSED, RETURNED. An active track records the resolver/custodian role (never a personal name), required evidence, evidence locator, review, decision/approval reference and affected artifact/phase. Canonical GAP status remains OPEN until the provenance and resolution discipline above permits RESOLVED or CLOSED; a track reaching RESOLVED, ACCEPTED_DEFERRAL or CLOSED requires that same discipline and an Owner decision reference. No track is active in this revision: every GAP-001–GAP-029 track is NOT_STARTED, and activation requires Owner assignment.
