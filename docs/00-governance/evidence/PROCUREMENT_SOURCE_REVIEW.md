## Review method and limits

All 23 PDFs in this review were opened and their 47 pages inspected through text extraction and structural/topic checks. All six DOCX files were opened and their paragraphs and tables inspected. Particular attention went to generic activity descriptions, responsibility lanes, approval metadata, and document labels. PDF page numbers below are physical, one-based pages. DOCX table, row, and column numbers are one-based; numbered process steps are distinguished from table rows.

The tender template page 2, original procurement diagram page 3, and borrowing SOP page 3 were also rendered and visually inspected to confirm the responsibility positions. Other PDF pages did not receive a complete visual/signature review. The DOCX documents were inspected structurally; no DOCX pagination or rendering is claimed. PDF and DOCX counterparts were inspected separately; their exact equivalence is not certified.

The two XLSX files and CSV were inspected read-only. All workbook sheets were traversed for structure and formula presence. Consultancy headers, shared-input and output sheet structure, and Flow Test label positions were inspected. Spreadsheet formulas were not recalculated, financial results were not reconciled, and output document pagination was not verified. Filled worksheet dimensions include formatting and blank scaffolding and are not package counts.

Only sanitized descriptions, safe filenames, role labels, and locators are retained here. Historical names, provider identities, document identifiers, contact details, account/tax identifiers, and monetary values are excluded. Raw documents remain under the ignored `reference-inputs/` path. No source file was modified or exported, and no raw file or extracted transcript was copied into tracked documentation.

## Evidence findings

### PR-F01 Template approval and version authority remain unverified

In each of the three procedural-template DOCX files, table 2 rows 1-4 have blank SOP number, creation date, revision date, and effective date. Row 5 contains an approver label, but that is not evidence of actual issuance or approval. The corresponding PDFs carry the same metadata subject. The borrowing SOP has blank metadata cells on page 1 as well. The service-standard files describe procedures, but an approved/effective version was not established through this inspection.

Classification: `OBSERVED_CURRENT_PROCESS` for document content; `UNRESOLVED_GAP` for official authority. Do not promote these files to `AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE` merely because they list legal references or an institutional approver.

### PR-F02 SPPBJ responsibility needs a precise activity distinction

The overall procurement template assigns the issuance/signing/contract-control activity to PPK at PDF page 2 step 14 and DOCX table 6 row 16, column 6. Its service-standard counterpart assigns SPPBJ establishment and contract signing to PPK at DOCX table 1 row 3 item i.

The tender template assigns preparation of contract and SPPBJ documents to Pokja at PDF page 2 step 11 and DOCX table 6 row 13, column 3. The original Bizagi tender diagram on page 3 similarly places preparation of contract and `SPBJ` under Pokja. The tender service-standard DOCX table 1 row 3 item l explicitly flags this lane versus establishment-responsibility question. The historical SPPBJ letter, page 1, contains a Pejabat Pembuat Komitmen label.

These sources may distinguish preparation from establishment/issuance and signature. They do not safely establish the complete responsibility split. The source's assertion about regulatory responsibility has not been independently verified. Classification: `OBSERVED_CURRENT_PROCESS` for these positions; `UNRESOLVED_GAP` for the binding split. Preserve both the spelling discrepancy (`SPBJ`/`SPPBJ`) and the activity distinction in later validation.

### PR-F03 RUP and organizational request handoffs are candidate evidence

The RUP template DOCX table 6 rows 3-8 and original diagram page 2 describe Unit Kerja/User, Perencanaan/Keuangan, PPK, Admin/Operator SiRUP, and KPA positions. The RUP service-standard DOCX table 1 row 3 describes return-for-correction and publication. Its table 1 row 4 also contains a dated publication-deadline claim.

Classification: `OBSERVED_CURRENT_PROCESS` for the described handoffs and deadline wording; `UNRESOLVED_GAP` for the official sequence, applicable deadline, and current organizational assignments. No source validates every faculty, directorate, institute, bureau, or future unit's central submission path. Owner decisions OD-04/OD-05 still require a generic organization hierarchy and validation of the official approval sequence.

The references to SiRUP/SPSE/LPSE show external-system context. They do not authorize an integration or make institutional access a V1 dependency. Owner decision OD-12 applies.

### PR-F04 Shared workbook inputs feed many document families

The consultancy workbook has 26 sheets, including two empty sheets (`Lembar3` and `Lembar1`). Its `Input!A2:KV2` contains 308 populated field headings. The field groups cover package/method information, formal process positions, procurement documents, provider data, guarantees, contracts, payment stages, addenda, inspection teams, BAST, and SPJ. `Isian Nomor!A2:AN2` is a related document-number header range. Formula-reference inspection confirms that `Mail Merge` reads `Input` and that multiple output sheets read `Mail Merge`, with additional dependencies between output sheets. Their values and computational correctness are not certified. An external-workbook reference is present at `SPJ PL!C1267`; its target identity/path is deliberately excluded and its availability/correctness was not validated.

CSV dimensions must not be interpreted as a count of populated procurement packages or an approved import contract.

### PR-F05 Historical procurement outputs need method-specific validation

Direct-procurement invitation, explanation, opening, evaluation, negotiation, result, winner, and SPPBJ artifacts provide examples of document relationships. `LAPORAN HASIL TENDER.pdf` page 1 combines a tender heading with direct-procurement wording. That observation prevents assuming that filenames alone define method applicability or that all output variants can be reused unchanged.

Classification: `OBSERVED_CURRENT_PROCESS` for artifact content; `UNRESOLVED_GAP` for method-specific output ownership and approved variants. The correspondence files also show technical-team/management coordination, but attendance or invitation role labels cannot define mandatory approval gates or global application roles. Owner decision OD-09 preserves that distinction.

### PR-F06 Borrowing sources have different procedural coverage

The borrowing service standard, page 1 first table row 3, describes online institutional submission, hierarchical dispositions, leadership decision, Kabag PSRT, administration staff, and coordination with room staff or driver. It does not enumerate the hierarchy. Its requirements on page 1 row 2 distinguish applicant recommendation contexts.

The borrowing SOP pages 3-4 gives a seven-step matrix with Pemohon, Kabag RT and Staf, availability checks, permission, preparation, BAST, use, and return inspection. It does not document the same disposition chain. These may describe different abstraction levels, scopes, or versions. The evidence does not establish equivalence or precedence.

Classification: `OBSERVED_CURRENT_PROCESS` for both descriptions; `UNRESOLVED_GAP` for official version, approval sequence, responsibility and V1 scope. An existing institutional web-submission route must not become a V1 dependency under OD-12. The presence of borrowing material also does not itself add borrowing functionality to approved V1 scope.
