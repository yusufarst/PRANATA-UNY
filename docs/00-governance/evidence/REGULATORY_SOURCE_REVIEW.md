## RG-01 Regime scope

| Topic | PR 34/2023 (SRC-048) | Perpres 16/2018 jo. 46/2025 (SRC-049/050) |
|---|---|---|
| Legal status of UNY | "UNY … adalah perguruan tinggi negeri badan hukum" (Pasal 1 angka 1); considers PP 35/2022 on PTN-BH UNY. | PTN-BH, universities as procuring entities and own revenue are **not mentioned**; "Perguruan Tinggi" appears only as a possible research implementer (16/2018 Pasal 62 ayat 3). |
| Funds covered | Procurement financed from non-APBN/APBD funds and from grants whose procurement is not otherwise regulated (Pasal 1 angka 15); all UNY procurement except activities whose procurement the funder regulates (Pasal 3). | Procurement by K/L/Perangkat Daerah financed from APBN/APBD (Pasal 2); 46/2025 adds "Institusi Lainnya" and Pemerintah Desa funded wholly or partly by APBN/APBD/APB Desa (Pasal 1 angka 5a; Pasal 2). |
| Exceptions | Funder-specific procurement rules (Pasal 3). | BLU/BLUD, published-tariff purchases, established business practice and procurement governed by other legislation (Pasal 61); PDN/UMK duties still apply (61 ayat 1a). |
| Budget document named | RBA UNY — annual business plan and budget (Pasal 1 angka 14). | DIPA/DPA (national/regional). |

**Planning consequence:** two candidate regimes exist. Which UNY funding source falls under which regime, and whether an APBN allocation to UNY makes it an "Institusi Lainnya", is a determination outside these texts (GAP-026). PRANATA therefore keeps regime as data on each package (DC-080) and never applies one regime's values to the other (BR-031).

## RG-03 Methods and values

Procurement types: PR 34/2023 uses *barang, pekerjaan konstruksi, jasa non konsultansi, jasa konsultansi*; the Perpres uses *Barang, Pekerjaan Konstruksi, Jasa Lainnya, Jasa Konsultansi*. PRANATA's four types (OD-32) map *Jasa Lainnya* to *jasa non konsultansi* under the PR 34/2023 regime; the label shown follows the regime.

| Method | PR 34/2023 criteria (SRC-048) | Perpres 16/2018 jo. 46/2025 (SRC-049/050) |
|---|---|---|
| Pengadaan Langsung | Barang/PK/non-konsultansi up to Rp700.000.000; konsultansi up to Rp300.000.000; and/or standard, low-risk, readily available, daily operational needs (Pasal 25). | Barang/JL up to Rp200.000.000; PK up to Rp400.000.000 (raised by 46/2025 from Rp200.000.000); JK up to Rp100.000.000 (Pasal 38 ayat 3; Pasal 41 ayat 3). |
| Quotation | Above Rp700.000.000 up to Rp2.000.000.000 (non-konsultansi); konsultansi above Rp300.000.000 up to Rp400.000.000; or risk/cost criteria (Pasal 26). Requires DPT; invite at least three DPT providers via pbj.uny.ac.id (Lampiran III B). | Not a Perpres method. |
| Tender | Above Rp2.000.000.000 (non-konsultansi); konsultansi above Rp300.000.000; complex/high-technology/new work; open, limited or two-stage (Pasal 27). | Tender and Tender cepat have no value floor; Tender is residual (Pasal 38 ayat 6–7). JK uses Seleksi above Rp100.000.000 (Pasal 41). |
| Penunjukan Langsung | *Keadaan tertentu* a–o and *kriteria khusus* a–g (Pasal 28). | Criteria a–k (Barang/PK/JL) and a–j (JK), plus Pasal 41A; post-qualification (46/2025 Pasal 38 ayat 5, 41 ayat 5, 44 ayat 3 huruf c). |
| E-Purchasing | Items in the UNY e-catalogue at e-market.uny.ac.id (Pasal 29); ceiling Rp15.000.000.000 (Lampiran III E angka 7); order, negotiation and Surat Pesanan. | Mandatory when the need is in the e-catalogue, with PPK-assessed exceptions (46/2025 Pasal 50 ayat 5–5c); JK added (Pasal 41 ayat 1 huruf a); "mini kompetisi" and "pembelian langsung" used but undefined. |
| Mandatory electronic system | Quotation invitations via pbj.uny.ac.id; tender lanes on LPSE observed in SRC-053. | Transactional e-procurement system mandatory for PL above Rp50.000.000, PnL, Tender cepat and Tender; for JK PL, PnL and Seleksi (Pasal 38 ayat 8; 41 ayat 7). |
| DPT | Providers must be registered in the DPT for Quotation, Tender and Penunjukan Langsung (Pasal 15); periodic evaluation and data updates (Pasal 16); registration, verification and updates at Subdit ALP (Lampiran I); removal procedure (Pasal 59). | No DPT; qualification via SIKaP/e-procurement systems (not detailed in the supplied text). |

**Planning consequence:** each regime becomes a separate versioned policy pack with its own value bands, criteria and citations (CAP-23). Overlapping or inconsistent PR 34/2023 bands are flagged, never auto-resolved (GAP-027).

## RG-06 Design implications recorded for P4–P11

1. Regime, method rules, value bands, document variants, segregation constraints and approval chains are **versioned policy data**, each citing its source article; nothing regulatory is hard-coded (CAP-23, ADR-0003).
2. A policy version becomes ACTIVE only by maker-checker activation with resolution evidence for the GAPs it depends on, and it is officially effective only within its effective window; outputs computed without a version officially effective on the action's reference date are labeled "Simulasi — kebijakan belum disahkan" (INV-022; [POLICY_REGISTRY §3.1](../../04-architecture/POLICY_REGISTRY.md#31-keberlakuan-resmi)).
3. The system suggests eligible methods with citations; an assigned official determines the method with a reason (BR-031); overlaps require an explicit choice (GAP-027).
4. Official external channels (SiRUP, LPSE/SPSE, e-catalogues, pbj.uny.ac.id, e-market.uny.ac.id) remain authoritative; PRANATA stores references and evidence and does not integrate in V1 (BR-027, GAP-029).
5. Both regimes' four procurement types are supported; the first Procurement UAT exercises them with synthetic data up to contract-document preparation (OD-32).
